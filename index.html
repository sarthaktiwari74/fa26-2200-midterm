An inclusive community makerspace

The ATLAS BTU Lab facilitates projects at the intersection of art, design, engineering and architecture. It serves as a general workspace and workshop host for an experimental community of makers.

Who can use BTU?

Anyone can gain access to BTU, although the lab currently cannot sponsor BuffOne Guest cards because of increased campus security.

How do I get access?

New members attend a 20-minute general orientation covering community expectations, safety and equipment. Some resources, including the wood shop and lasers, require additional orientations or training.

Materials and reciprocity

BTU is not a free supply store. The lab encourages reciprocity through displaying work, tagging BTU on social media, offering workshops, helping organize supplies and other contributions to the community.
