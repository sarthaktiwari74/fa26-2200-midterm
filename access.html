Tools

General tools and materials have green labels. BTU provides shared workspace and tools for making and experimenting.

  • Knives, cutting mats, scissors for non-fabric use and measuring tools
  • Glues, hot glue guns, tapes, zip ties and other fasteners
  • Screwdrivers, drills, drivers and Dremel tools
  • Saws, files, chisels, sandpaper and sanding tools
  • Paper, fabric and supplies for two-dimensional and three-dimensional crafts
  • Plywood and plastics suitable for laser cutting

When using shared materials, contribute to the community. You can display your work, credit BTU online, teach a workshop or help organize supplies.

Woodshop

The entrance has pink markings; equipment inside uses its own label colors. Woodshop access requires a separate staff orientation. Register through the calendar.

Mischief Makers support machine access. Members can also become Mischief Makers.

  • Saws: table, band, chop and hand saws
  • Drill press, drills, drivers and palm router
  • Belt and random-orbit sanders, files and measuring tools

Shop rules:

  • The table saw, band saw, drill press, chop saw and belt sander are switched off from 10 p.m. to 6 a.m. Never interfere with timers.
  • Follow every safety rule, even when tired or facing deadlines. Keep someone within hearing distance.
  • Ask for help with unfamiliar equipment. Do not permanently modify machines or remove tools without staff permission.
  • Report faulty equipment instead of trying to repair it.

Violations remove access until both lab and woodshop orientations are repeated. Staff can revoke access when necessary; repeated violations can restrict access for everyone.

Lasers

Red labels identify laser equipment and materials. General lab access does not include laser access; a separate orientation is required.

  • A 75-watt CO2 laser with a 30-by-18-inch bed (762 by 457 mm)
  • A 75-watt CO2 laser with a 40-by-28-inch bed (1016 by 711 mm)
  • Materials include plywood, plastics, cardboard, paper and fabric

Getting access:

  1. Attend a laser orientation listed on the calendar.
  2. For class assignments, ask your instructor, TA or LA for help first.
  3. Arrange guided sessions with a Lazor Wizard while you still need assistance.
  4. Demonstrate correct, independent operation to gain full access.
  5. Reserve one-hour slots on the whiteboard beside the laser, outside orientation times.

The official page lists Lazor Wizard contacts. Use the email subject “Lazor Wizards” when contacting the group.

3D Printers

Purple labels identify 3D printing equipment. Print jobs are handled in arrival order. Check with staff for current hardware and software procedures.

  • Three Prusa Mk4 printers
  • Three Prusa Mk3S+ printers
  • Four Prusa MINI2 printers
  • Additional printers for experiments arranged with staff
  • 1.75 mm PLA and ABS filament in black and assorted colors

Printing guidelines:

  • Get training from staff rather than teaching yourself to use the machines.
  • Use the provided Prusa Slicer setup for each printer.
  • Leave the machines unmodified.
  • Get staff approval before using materials other than PLA or ABS.
  • Work directly with staff to start prints while the lab updates its file management system.
  • Coordinate class assignments with instructors, TAs or LAs before requesting additional help from Mischief Makers.

Use the main-space printers for their intended purpose. Ask a Mischief Maker about experimental projects, and check the calendar for printing workshops.
