Faculty Resources

Supporting coursework, research and experimentation

BTU says it supports as many as 7,000 coursework hours per semester. Because of that demand, students and faculty use brief, mandatory, one-time orientations for relevant resources.

Teaching a class in BTU

Faculty teaching an upcoming class that uses the lab are asked to complete the official semester form. Faculty should also plan for resource-specific training and instructional requirements.

Resource planning

Laser cutting

Laser access requires a one-time orientation, with schedules listed on the BTU calendar. Course assignments should be facilitated by instructors, LAs or TAs first.

3D printing

BTU describes a one-time 10–15 minute orientation for 3D printing users, available for individuals or classes by request.

Wood shop

Wood shop access requires a one-time orientation regardless of prior experience.

Faculty contact

Director Zack Weaver is the listed contact for faculty questions. The official faculty page also contains the current course-use form and detailed instructional documents.
